AI Features

Generate / Append Builds or appends full knowledge graphs with nodes and directed lines using the selected AI model. 1-Year Roadmap Creates timeline-aware concepts mapped directly across the 12-month calendar overlay. Expand Selected Select 1 node and click "🌱 Expand Selected Node" to branch out sub-topics. Connect 2 Nodes Select 2 nodes to find and create missing relationship links or bridge concepts. Shortcuts & Touch

Pan & Zoom Two-finger pinch to zoom; drag empty space to pan. Ctrl+Wheel zooms. Draw Connections Drag from near a node towards another node to link them, or use the bottom-right arrow ball. Delete Press Del or Backspace with nodes or lines selected.
