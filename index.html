Concept Map Guide

Drawing Arrows

Proximity Arrow Click and drag near a node (outside its bounds) towards another node to grow and steer a live connection arrow. Release over the target node to create the line.

Touch Gestures

Pan Drag one finger on empty space away from any node, or drag two fingers anywhere. Zoom Pinch with two fingers, or use the + / − buttons (tap the % to reset to 100%). Ctrl/Cmd + mouse wheel also zooms. Edit / open Tap an already-selected box (without dragging) to edit it or open its link.
