Format Notes

TXT Simple: one node per line as id text [url] x y, then connections as -1 fromId toId (arrow) or -2 fromId toId (plain line).
TXT Indent: an outline; each indent level (tab, 4 spaces, or 2 spaces) becomes a child of the line above it.
